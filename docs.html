This page explains what the tool does and how to use every panel. It's a user-facing guide -- for the data-sourcing methodology (what's verified against real v62 Skill.wz/Item.wz data vs. estimated) see the project README.

What this is

A calculator for evaluating MapleStory (classic, pre-Big-Bang v62) training spots: pick your class and skill, enter your stats, and the monster list ranks every monster by realistic EXP/hour and mesos/hour -- accounting for how many casts it actually takes to kill something, MP (and optionally HP) potion costs, and how good each monster's spawn map actually is for your specific skill's hitbox.

Class & Skill selection

At the top of the app: a row of class chips (Warrior Magician Bowman Thief Pirate) plus Show All Skills. Clicking a class chip narrows the skill row below it to just that class's kit -- e.g. clicking Thief shows only Lucky Seven, Drain, and Savage Blow. This is a display filter only: it doesn't change which skill is currently active, so you can browse a class's options without losing your place.

The skill row itself is what actually drives every number on the page -- damage formula, MP cost, cast time, and hits/targets per cast are all specific to whichever skill chip is selected (highlighted purple). Every selectable skill has a real, sourced v62 formula behind it (mostly straight from a Skill.wz data dump); a skill shown greyed-out with [soon] has no verified formula yet and can't be selected.

Heal (Cleric) is a normal skill choice like any other -- select it to see its own damage/cast numbers. Its own controls (skill level, undead-per-cast) only appear below once Heal is the active skill.

Character panel

Click CHARACTER Lv.. [edit] to expand your stat inputs: Level, STR/DEX/INT/LUK, Weapon Attack, Max MP, Max HP, and Defense. These feed directly into the active skill's damage formula and into the incoming-damage estimate (see "Take Damage" below). "Reset to defaults" restores the app's baseline test character.

Combat stats (per monster)

FieldMeaning
CASTSHow many casts of your active skill it takes to kill this monster. 1 = one-shot.
YOUR DMGYour damage range per cast (and per individual hit, if the skill fires multiple hits/bullets per cast).
MP/KILLTotal MP spent killing one of this monster, net of MP Eater's expected return if you've set an MP Eater level.
ONE-SHOT @The character level at which your current build would one-shot this monster, if you're not one-shotting it already.

If the monster has an elemental weakness/resistance/immunity and your active skill is elemental (Fire Arrow, Poison Breath, Cold Beam, Thunder Bolt), damage is automatically scaled (1.5x weak, 0.5x resist, 0x immune) and shown next to YOUR DMG.

Filters

Search by name/location/element, level range, and a row of type toggles (Boss/Undead/Auto). "Hide Rope-Heavy Maps" and "Hide Low-Spawn Maps" hide a monster only if every one of its known spawn maps has that problem -- a monster with even one good map stays visible. Zone, elemental-weakness, and efficiency-tier dropdowns narrow further. "Min Map Score" (see below) filters on the active skill's own map-quality score.

General controls

MP Eater

Models the passive's chance-based MP return per hit against the target monster's own MP pool -- 0 disables it.

Session & Potion

Session length (minutes) and which MP potion you'd buy -- both feed the Session Profit panel on every monster card.

Take Damage ON by default

Models incoming damage from the monster and the HP potions needed to sustain it: roughly one hit every "Hit Interval" seconds (3-5s, adjustable) for max(1, monster W.ATK - your Defense) each time. Warriors are melee and always effectively in range for this; other classes only take it if you expect to be mobbed or pulling AoE packs. Turn it off to model taking no damage at all. When on, pick which HP potion you'd buy.

This is an explicit estimate, not a sourced hit-chance/defense formula -- real MapleStory accuracy/avoidability and defense mechanics weren't reverse-engineered for this. Treat the resulting HP-potion numbers as a reasonable approximation, not gospel.

Magic Guard Magician skills only

Only appears when a Magician skill is active and Take Damage is on. Magic Guard converts a % of incoming damage into MP loss instead of HP loss (2% per level, capping at 40% at level 20) -- sourced directly from v62 skill data. Raising the slider trades HP-potion cost for MP-potion cost; because MP potions and HP potions have different mesos-per-point-restored rates, this can raise or lower your total session cost depending on which potions you've selected.

Session Profit panel (per monster)

Projects a full session at your chosen length: total kills, potion cost (split into MP and, when relevant, HP pots), mesos income (from real per-monster drop tables, not a flat constant), net profit, and levels/EXP gained.

Map training scores

Every monster's known spawn maps get a 1-5 score, colored on a genuine red-to-green gradient (■■■■■ 1=worst, 5=best) -- which score you see depends on your active skill's own hitbox shape:

BadgeApplies toRewards
RANGERanged single-target skills (Magic Claw, Double Shot, Lucky Seven, Drain, elemental spells, etc.)Long platforms, few floors, good mob density
MELEEShort-reach single-target skills (Power Strike, Double Uppercut)Same shape as RANGE with a much shorter reach constant
AOESame-platform multi-mob skills (Slash Blast, Somersault Kick, Thunder Bolt, etc.)Mobs clumped tightly on one platform (or a close neighboring one)
STACKHeal onlyPlatforms stacked close together vertically -- Heal's box is wide enough to hit several floors in one cast

Two independent penalties can dock a map's score: a ROPE-HEAVY badge (real rope/ladder travel tedium between farming floors) and a LOW SPAWN badge (too few monsters alive at once to sustain continuous farming). A NO PORTAL PATH badge means the portal graph couldn't confirm a walkable route from any town -- a strong signal, not certainty (NPC-dialogue-triggered maps like most Party Quests have no portal edge at all and will show this even though they're real content).

Map view

Click a spawn-map thumbnail to expand it: toggle overlays for real monster spawn points ("Mob Spawns"), rope/ladder segments, and clickable portals (jump to the target map without closing the modal; "Back" retraces your steps). The world-map view shows the same map's position on its regional overview.

Data accuracy, in short

  • STATS VERIFIED monsters were individually spot-checked against a second source; NORMAL monsters come from the same verified dump but weren't individually re-checked.
  • Every selectable skill's damage formula is sourced from real v62 Skill.wz data or a verified v62-era private-server implementation -- not guessed.
  • Incoming damage/HP-potion modeling (see "Take Damage" above) and map-quality scoring constants are clearly-labeled estimates/community-standard values, not individually wz-verified the same way skill damage is.
